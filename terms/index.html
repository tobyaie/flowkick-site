2. Playing safely

Freestyle football is physical. Practise in a safe, clear space, away from roads, people and things that can break, and stop if anything hurts. Young players should practise with a grown-up's say-so. Touch counts, ratings and trick results are estimates made by the app, not judgements by a coach or referee.
